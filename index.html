Mental Math Mastery Tips

Professional shortcuts to calculate multiplication in milliseconds.

Multiplying by 9

Multiply the number by 10, then subtract the number itself.

Example: 19 × 9 = (19 × 10) − 19 = 190 − 19 = 171

Multiplying by 11

For two-digit numbers (ab × 11), separate digits and insert their sum (a + b) in the middle.

Example: 14 × 11 → (1)(1+4)(4) = 154

Multiplying by 25

Multiply by 100, then divide by 4.

Example: 24 × 25 = (24 × 100) ÷ 4 = 2400 ÷ 4 = 600

Splitting Numbers (Distributive)

Break hard multipliers into friendly tens and units.

Example: 18 × 7 = (20 × 7) − (2 × 7) = 140 − 14 = 126
